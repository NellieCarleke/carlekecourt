Frequently asked questions

How should I film my match?

Film in landscape from behind the back wall, as high up as you can, with the whole court in view. Record one game per video. After each analysis the app rates the recording, so you can see what to improve next time.

How do I analyze a match?

Record the game with your iPhone, then bring the video into CarlekeCourt from your photo library. The app follows both players, splits the game into rallies and keeps the score.

Does my video get uploaded anywhere?

No. All analysis happens on your iPhone. Your videos and stats never leave your phone unless you choose to share a match yourself.

The app mixed up the players. What do I do?

Tell the app who is who once, and every stat is shown from your side.

A point is missing or wrong.

You can add or fix points yourself in a few taps from the match.

How do I share a match with my opponent or coach?

Use AirDrop to send an analyzed match. They get the video with every rally and stat.

Why does the app ask for access to my photos?

To play and analyze your match videos straight from your photo library, and to save videos of matches that others send you. The camera is only used if you take a photo for a match.

How do I delete my data?

Everything is stored on your iPhone. Deleting a match in the app removes it, and deleting the app removes all of its data. Your original videos stay in your photo library.

Which devices are supported?

CarlekeCourt runs on iPhone with iOS 26 or later.
