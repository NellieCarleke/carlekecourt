Support Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

No data leaves your phone

CarlekeCourt is built so that you stay in control of your data. We do not collect, store, sell or see any of your information. There is no account, no sign-up and no tracking.

What stays on your iPhone

The app analyzes your match videos entirely on your device. The results, such as rallies, scores, statistics, tags and player profiles, are stored only on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

Permissions

Photos: used to play and analyze match videos from your photo library, and to save videos of matches that others send you.

Camera: used only if you choose to take a photo for a match.

You can change these permissions at any time in the Settings app on your iPhone.

Sharing

A match only leaves your phone if you choose to share it yourself, for example with AirDrop. You decide what you share and with whom.

No analytics, ads or third parties

CarlekeCourt contains no analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and makes no network connections. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports. This is handled by Apple and can be turned off in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

Deleting your data

Because all data is stored on your iPhone, you can remove it at any time by deleting matches in the app or by deleting the app. Your original videos remain in your photo library.

Children

CarlekeCourt does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
